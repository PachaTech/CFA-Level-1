## Where to start with the changes

1. **Quantitative Methods.** Work the cash-flow-weighted return example, then reproduce the minimum-variance mix and the spreadsheet simulations. The 2027 core reading and its online Equation Explorers cover time value of money. The former Quant prerequisite has been retired. [Quant review](Secret%20Sauce/Quants/SS%20Quantative%20Methods.ipynb)

2. **Equities.** Follow one company from unit sales to cash flow and value per share. Change its margin under a downside scenario, then probability-weight the valuations. Continue to the research report and factor-model example. [Forecast valuation](Equity%20Investments/Module%2048.2/Module%2048.2%20Study%20-%20Dividend%20Discount%20Models.ipynb) · [Research and relative valuation](Equity%20Investments/Module%2048.3/Module%2048.3%20-%20Relative%20Valuation%20Measures.ipynb)

3. **Ethical and Professional Standards.** Work a case for each of Standards I–VII using the 12th edition. Review competence, communication of client services and costs, and how to avoid or disclose conflicts. [Ethics cases](Ethical%20and%20Professional%20Standards/Module%2091/Module%2091.1%20-%20Guidance%20for%20Standards.ipynb) · [CFA Institute handbook](https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards)
